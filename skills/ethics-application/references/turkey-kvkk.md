# Turkey: ethics committee and KVKK reference

Use this file when the application is for a Turkish institution. It is orientation, **not legal advice**. Last checked 2026-11-07 against official texts (sources are given per item). Laws and regulations are amended often, and this check read one consolidated text of the law as served on that date, so confirm everything with the institution's ethics committee secretariat and its data-protection contact before submitting. Items marked *(verify)* were **not** checked against a primary source.

## Committee and forms

- Retrospective, observational, chart-review studies on humans typically go to a **Non-Interventional Clinical Research Ethics Committee** (Turkish: *Girişimsel Olmayan Klinik Araştırmalar Etik Kurulu*). Checked only at the level of one example: the Ankara Medipol University directive for that committee defines non-interventional research to include "dosya ve görüntü kayıtları gibi retrospektif arşiv taramaları" (retrospective archive reviews such as file and image records). That directive names the Ministry of Health's *Klinik Araştırmalar Hakkında Yönetmelik* (Resmî Gazete 13 April 2013, No. 28617) as its basis; that regulation was **not read** and its current status was not checked, and other university directives cite different dates. Committee names, scope and forms are institution-specific *(verify for your institution)*.
- Ask the user for their committee's directive (*yönerge*) and current application form. They decide the structure and level of detail; use them in committee mode (see `../SKILL.md`, step 3).
- Training and research hospitals, university hospitals and some private hospitals have their own form and meeting calendar *(verify for your institution)*.
- Interventional drug/device studies follow different regulatory routes and are outside this skill.
- Many institutions also require administrative permission (for example from the hospital management) to access patient records for research *(verify)*.
- Forms are usually filled in **Turkish** *(verify)*. Write the application in Turkish if the institution requires it, and keep the clinical terms in their usual English/Latin form where physicians customarily do.

## Personal data law (KVKK, Law No. 6698)

Source for the items in this section unless stated: the consolidated text of Law No. 6698 on mevzuat.gov.tr (adopted 24 March 2016, Resmî Gazete 7 April 2016, No. 29677), which shows the amendments of Law No. 7499 (2 March 2024; its changes to Articles 6 and 9 took effect 1 June 2024).

- **Health data is special-category data.** Article 6(1) lists "sağlığı" (health) among the *özel nitelikli kişisel veri*.
- **Grounds for processing special-category data (Article 6(3), as amended in 2024).** Processing is prohibited unless one of these applies: explicit consent; an express provision of law; protection of life or bodily integrity of someone unable to consent; data made public by the data subject; establishment, exercise or protection of a right; processing by persons under a confidentiality duty or by authorised bodies for public health, preventive medicine, medical diagnosis, treatment and care, and the planning, management and financing of health services; employment, occupational health and safety, social security and social services obligations; and certain non-profit bodies' processing of their members' data. **None of these grounds names scientific research.** Which route a retrospective study relies on is a question for the ethics committee and the data-protection contact, not for this skill.
- **Additional measures (Article 6(4)).** Processing special-category data also requires the adequate measures set by the Personal Data Protection Board. These are in Board Decision 2018/10 of 31 January 2018 (kvkk.gov.tr). Its measures include: a separate security policy and procedure for special-category data; regular training; confidentiality agreements; clearly defined and periodically checked access authority, removed immediately on role change or departure; encrypted storage with keys kept on a separate medium; secure logging of operations; at least two-step authentication for remote access; physical security; encrypted transfer (corporate e-mail address or KEP for e-mail, encrypted portable media with the key kept separately, VPN or sFTP between servers). The decision also points to the Authority's Personal Data Security Guide, which was not read.
- **Research provisions.**
  - Article 28(1)(b): the law does not apply to personal data processed "araştırma, planlama ve istatistik gibi amaçlarla" (for purposes such as research, planning and statistics) after being anonymized.
  - Article 28(1)(c): the law does not apply to processing for artistic, historical, literary or scientific purposes, or within freedom of expression, provided that national defence, security, public order, economic security, privacy or personality rights are not violated and no crime is constituted.
  - The Personal Health Data Regulation (*Kişisel Sağlık Verileri Hakkında Yönetmelik*, Resmî Gazete 21 June 2019, No. 30808, Article 16, "Bilimsel amaçlarla işleme") applies those two paragraphs to health data. First, scientific work may be done on health data that the data controller has anonymized under Article 28(1)(b). Second, health data may be processed for scientific purposes under Article 28(1)(c), within technical and administrative measures, if privacy and personality rights are not violated and no crime is constituted. This regulation replaced the 2016 health-data regulation (Resmî Gazete 20 October 2016, No. 29863), which no longer applies (Article 23 of the 2019 regulation). Source: resmigazete.gov.tr.
- **Anonymized is not the same as coded.** Law Article 3(1)(b) defines anonymization as making data impossible to link to an identified or identifiable person *even by matching with other data*. The Regulation on Deletion, Destruction or Anonymization of Personal Data (Resmî Gazete 28 October 2017, No. 30224, Article 10) adds that data counts as anonymous only if the controller, the recipient or recipient groups cannot re-identify it with suitable techniques such as reversal or matching with other datasets (read through a summary of the article, not the full text; quote the article itself in a protocol). The law text contains no separate term for pseudonymization. On this definition, a dataset with a study code and a retained key is *not* anonymized; this is a reading of the definition, not a quoted rule.
- **Consent.** Many retrospective, de-identified chart-review studies are handled with a waiver of consent, but the **committee and institutional policy decide** *(verify)*. Do not state that consent is "not needed"; write what the institution's decision process will be.
- **Transfer abroad (Article 9, rewritten in 2024).** Personal data may be transferred abroad if an Article 5 or 6 ground exists and an adequacy decision covers the country, sector or international organisation. Without an adequacy decision, one of the listed appropriate safeguards is needed (for example Board-approved binding corporate rules, standard contracts, or an undertaking), and only if the data subject can exercise rights and obtain effective remedies there. If neither exists, only *occasional* (*arızi*) transfers on listed grounds are allowed, which include explicit consent after being informed of the possible risks. Onward transfers are covered too (Article 9(8)). The implementing regulation is the *Kişisel Verilerin Yurt Dışına Aktarılmasına İlişkin Usul ve Esaslar Hakkında Yönetmelik* (Resmî Gazete 10 July 2024, No. 32598, in force on publication), which requires the Board's adequate measures for special-category data in the safeguard instruments (Articles 11, 13, 14, 15). Using cloud services or foreign-hosted LLM endpoints with patient-level data can therefore raise a transfer question. Whether a given endpoint's processing counts as a transfer is for the data-protection contact to decide. Data that is truly anonymized is outside the law under Article 28(1)(b).

## Practical checklist

- Direct identifiers (name, national ID number, phone) are absent from the analysis dataset; a study-specific code is used and the linkage key is stored separately with restricted access.
- The application describes the storage and access controls with Board Decision 2018/10 in mind (encryption, key separation, access logging, two-step remote authentication).
- Free-text notes are never sent to external services unless the institution has approved it and the text is de-identified.
- The application states who has access, where data are stored, for how long, and how they are destroyed.
- If an LLM is used on notes, the application says where the model runs and what leaves the institution.
